## Problem

Make social issues approachable for a generation less used to reading news: one link on a phone, and you get just the essentials.

## What I did

Planning, the AI summarization pipeline, and the Django backend. The frontend is React. Paste an article link into the search box and you get the summary plus a list of your recent summaries.

## Result

**Excellence Award**, 1st Konkuk University Hackathon (Konkuk University SW-centered University Project).
