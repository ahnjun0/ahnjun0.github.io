Goes beyond MBTI to match on sleep patterns, noise sensitivity, and similar habits. Inspired by the "hire a coworker for 50,000 won" meme, the **60,000-won allocation game** has you bet virtual money on the living conditions you care about to set weights, and the house rules that are awkward to bring up are settled contract-style and saved as a PDF. A KAIST EE teammate built the backend; I built the frontend.
